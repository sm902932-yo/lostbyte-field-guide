Ep02: How Does ChatGPT Actually Work?

ChatGPT chops text into tokens [Ep02 S1], places them on a map of meaning [Ep02 S3], highlights what matters with attention [Ep02 S4], and guesses the next token — a skill learned from next-word training [Ep02 S5] and polished by human feedback [Ep02 S9].
