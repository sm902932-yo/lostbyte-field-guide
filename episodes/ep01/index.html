Ep01: Why Does ChatGPT Make Things Up?

Chatbots predict likely words one token at a time and don’t look facts up unless connected to a source [Ep01 S2] [Ep01 S3]. Training and testing have rewarded confident guessing [Ep01 S2]. Fight back: allow “I don’t know”, give real sources, ask for quotes, verify specifics [Ep01 S8].
