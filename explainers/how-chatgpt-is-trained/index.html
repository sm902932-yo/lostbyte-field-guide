Episode 02

How ChatGPT is trained

Plain answer

Training is next-word prediction on large text: hide the next word, let the model guess, then nudge its settings toward the right answer [Ep02 S5]. GPT-3 (2020) had 175 billion of those settings, called parameters [Ep02 S5]. Performance improves predictably with model size, data and compute [Ep02 S6].

Byte & Bit analogy

Byte pictures a mixing desk covered in tiny knobs. Each round of the fill-in-the-blank game nudges the knobs a little. GPT-3 had 175 billion knobs.
