Episode 01

How to spot a made-up AI answer

Plain answer

In a 2023 study, researchers checked 636 citations ChatGPT generated for literature reviews on 42 topics: 55% of GPT-3.5’s citations and 18% of GPT-4’s were fabricated [Ep01 S6]. Treat those numbers as a 2023 snapshot. Watch for specific details you didn’t ask for, search for every citation yourself, and verify citations before trusting them [Ep01 S6] [Ep01 S8].

Byte & Bit analogy

Bit pasted a chatbot’s citation into an essay and went to make toast. Next time, Bit searched the title first, found no such paper, and picked a real study from the library database instead. Essay saved.
