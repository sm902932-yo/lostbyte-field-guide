Episode 01

Why do chatbots guess instead of saying “I don’t know”?

Plain answer

Next-word pretraining has no true/false labels, and tests that only score accuracy reward guessing over saying “I don’t know” [Ep01 S2]. OpenAI researchers compare it to a multiple-choice test: a model that guesses an unknown birthday has about a 1-in-365 chance of being right, and zero if it abstains [Ep01 S2]. Rare, one-off facts can’t be learned from patterns alone [Ep01 S2], though models often carry some signal about their own uncertainty [Ep01 S5].

Byte & Bit analogy

Byte compares it to a quiz where a blank answer scores zero. Bit figures a lucky guess beats nothing, so Bit always guesses, confidently. That is exactly the habit accuracy-only grading teaches.
