Episode 01 + 02

Next-token prediction, explained

Plain answer

Language models are trained to predict the next token on large amounts of text [Ep01 S3]. When answering, the model scores possible next tokens, picks a likely one, adds it, and repeats until done — it does not stop to check a database of facts [Ep01 S2] [Ep01 S3].

Byte & Bit analogy

Byte’s analogy: super-powered autocomplete. Or an improv actor handed a first line, keeping the scene going one sentence at a time with no hidden script.
