Episode 01 + 02

Human feedback coaching (RLHF)

Plain answer

Human testers compare model outputs and the model is tuned toward the preferred ones [Ep02 S8] [Ep02 S9]. In OpenAI’s InstructGPT study, people preferred a 1.3B-parameter coached model over the 175B GPT-3 — 100× fewer parameters [Ep02 S9]. Human-feedback training improved truthfulness and instruction following [Ep01 S7].

Byte & Bit analogy

Bit used to ramble on like a text continuer. Then a coach watched pairs of answers, picked the better one each time, and Bit learned to actually answer the question.
