Episode 02

Attention: the highlighter

Plain answer

Attention lets the model weigh which earlier tokens matter most for the next prediction; it is the core of the Transformer design published by Google researchers in 2017 [Ep02 S4].

Byte & Bit analogy

“Bank” — river or money? Bit runs a highlighter over the sentence: “fishing” means river, “loan” means money. That highlighter only works on what’s in front of it, so give it context.
