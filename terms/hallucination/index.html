Episode 01

What is an AI hallucination?

Plain answer

Researchers call it a hallucination when an AI produces something that sounds fluent and believable but is false, or isn’t supported by the source it was given [Ep01 S1]. The key part is the confidence: a hallucination reads just as smooth as a correct answer, with no warning label [Ep01 S1] [Ep01 S2].

Byte & Bit analogy

Bit asked a chatbot who wrote a famous paper on sleep and memory. It answered instantly with an author, a journal, a year, even a page number. The paper never existed. Byte’s verdict: confident fiction.
