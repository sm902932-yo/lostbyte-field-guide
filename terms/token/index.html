What is a token?

Plain answer

A token is a small piece of text: a whole word, part of a word, or punctuation [Ep01 S3]. OpenAI’s rough rule for English is 1 token ≈ ¾ of a word, so 100 tokens ≈ 75 words [Ep02 S1]. Rare or long words get split into sub-word pieces [Ep02 S2].

Byte & Bit analogy

Byte calls tokens “tiles”, like letter tiles in a word game. A short common word is one tile; a long or unusual word might be two or three. Bit reads, and writes, in tiles.
