Word maps (embeddings): king − man + woman

Plain answer

Each word gets a position in a meaning space where similar words sit close together [Ep02 S3]. In 2013, researchers showed that starting at “king”, subtracting “man” and adding “woman” lands closest to “queen” [Ep02 S3].

Byte & Bit analogy

Byte draws a giant invisible map: dog near puppy, Paris near London. Nobody drew it by hand — it formed from reading lots of text.
