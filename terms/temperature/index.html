Why ChatGPT gives different answers (temperature)

Plain answer

Always picking the single most likely word makes text bland and repetitive, so chatbots add some controlled randomness when choosing the next word [Ep02 S7]. That’s why asking twice can give two different answers. (The setting that controls this is commonly called temperature.)

Byte & Bit analogy

Bit asks “name my new cat” twice: Captain Whiskers, then Professor Paws. That’s the weighted dice roll. Add context — grey, grumpy, loves keyboards — and the answers fit: Sir Typo. (Dramatized mock-up.)
