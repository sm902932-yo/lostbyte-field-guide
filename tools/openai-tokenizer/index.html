OpenAI Tokenizer (free, no login)

Plain answer

The OpenAI Tokenizer is a public page, no login needed, that colours in every token of text you type [Ep02 S13]. “Why does ChatGPT make things up?” is 32 characters and 8 tokens: Why| does| Chat|GPT| make| things| up|? [Ep02 S13]. Its rule of thumb: about 4 characters, or ¾ of a word, per token for common English text [Ep02 S13] [Ep02 S1].
